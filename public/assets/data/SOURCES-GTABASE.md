## Data policy
- Each entry should retain its source URL and a verification/status label where possible.
- Unknown prices, performance values, release details and character statistics must remain unpublished rather than inferred.
- GTABase is a secondary reference. Its counts and records can change; check the linked source for the latest version.
- Images are included only where their origin and usage terms can be established. Rockstar trademarks and imagery remain the property of their respective owners.
